Questions owners ask

The questions we hear most.

What kinds of businesses does CDH Group buy? +

We focus on essential, everyday businesses — home services (garage doors, pest control, residential cleaning), commercial services (vent cleaning, electrical contracting), waste & hauling, and managed IT / MSP. In short: profitable, durable companies built on reputation and repeat customers.

Do you keep the existing team and management? +

Yes. The people who built the business keep building it. We grow what already works and quietly fix what doesn't — no slash-and-burn, no outside playbook dropped on people who already know their craft.

How is this different from private equity or a search fund? +

CDH Group isn't private equity. There's no fund behind it, no investors waiting to be paid back on a schedule, and no five-year exit looming. It's permanent capital — owned and run for the long term — which changes every decision that follows.

Will you change the company name or brand? +

No. The name stays on the door. The reputation you built is a big part of what makes the business valuable, and we intend to keep it exactly as it is.

How long do you hold a business after buying it? +

Indefinitely. We buy planning to keep — there's no exit on the calendar. That long horizon lets us invest patiently in the people, products, and relationships that already win.
