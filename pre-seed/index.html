The thesis

Most pre-seed money is already counting down to the next round. We back founders planning to build something that lasts.

We're not a fund with investors waiting to be paid back on a schedule. We're patient capital — the same conviction behind everything CDH does. That frees us to be early, to back real businesses in unglamorous industries, and to judge a pre-seed bet on the people and the problem rather than a pitch-deck hockey stick. If you're building something durable and useful, you shouldn't have to dress it up as the next unicorn to get your first check.

Founder questions

What founders ask us.

What stage do you invest at? +

Pre-seed — often the first outside check a founder takes. We're comfortable being early, before the metrics are obvious, when it's really a bet on the people and the problem.

What do you mean by "real-economy" founders? +

We're drawn to founders building durable, useful businesses in the everyday economy — services, operations, hardware, the unglamorous industries people actually depend on — not just the latest consumer app. It's the same conviction behind the rest of what we do.

How is this different from a typical pre-seed VC? +

We're patient capital, not a fund racing a clock. There's no pressure to raise again on someone else's timeline or to engineer a quick exit. We can back a founder for the long haul, through rounds and pivots.

Do you lead rounds, and what's your check size? +

We stay flexible and shape it around the founder and the round rather than forcing a template. The honest answer is: tell us what you're building and we'll tell you quickly how we can help. Start a conversation and we'll be direct about fit and size.

How fast is your process? +

Fast and honest. We make decisions quickly, give real answers instead of stringing you along, and won't drag you through endless partner meetings. A clear no beats a slow maybe.
